Let a live deck ask your System One model, such as TypeSafe's Jev, typed questions and transcribe speech through your saved Redcode connections, so it can show real-time feedback such as the speaker's mood without any key in its files. The design asks for each grant, and `window.redcode` offers `decide`, `transcribe` and `listen` in the review and while presenting.
