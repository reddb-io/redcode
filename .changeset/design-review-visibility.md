The Design review page now says why the agent is waiting or stopped: compacting its context, a scheduled retry with its countdown, a permission to approve in the terminal, a failed turn with its error, or an interruption; a cancelled message no longer stays queued. Revisions carry a number (R7) on the revision chip, the line above the preview, the picker, the activity feed and the design_preview and design_history results, and the agent names it in its reply. A refused message can be discarded or reloaded and resent, Send includes the text of an open note card, Restore asks first and waits while the agent works, the phone frame switch only previews while a new Details control sets the design's platform, and Audit, Slides to PDF, Present and the slide thumbnails follow the selected variant.
