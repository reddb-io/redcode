Design now finishes what it starts. When the Design agent stops after doing some work while a Design task is still pending or the design has no published revision yet, it is sent back to the work up to three times per message. Each reminder lists the pending tasks, and the wording changes once a revision is published. A reply that only talks, such as answering a question, ends it. An unfinished feedback round is now continued up to three times while each continuation is followed by progress. Each continuation names only the next missing step, together with the open notes. With single reasoning (the default), a message typed in the terminal, the app or an editor while a published design is open is now tracked as a review note without asking System One. The anti-slop audit no longer runs automatically at the end of a round. It runs when the reviewer asks for it, or before approval when `design.gate` requires it, and its findings are reported rather than fixed. Every refused note status now says it can instead be recorded unresolved or accepted with a reason, and offers partial once a verify failed the note. The Design prompt states the round rules as six numbered steps, in the same words as the review message.
