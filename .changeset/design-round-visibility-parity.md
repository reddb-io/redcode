Design review rounds now look the same in the review page, the terminal sidebar and the app's Design tab. The terminal sidebar keeps the newest round after every note has an outcome as one line (`Round 3 · 12 resolved · 1 partial · 1 unresolved`) and lists the partial and unresolved notes under it, names the revision under review (R7) and says when the review ends after this round, and folds the Design tasks into one line while a review has rounds. The app's Design tab shows the newest round's outcome counts, each note's outcome, the revision number and a pending end. The terminal and app feedback and approval cards name the revision (R7) when the agent's publish announced it. On the review page, Send & end is offered while earlier notes are still open as long as the message carries a note, and the round line says "Ending after this round" once it was requested.
