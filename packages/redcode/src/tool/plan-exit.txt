Finish the plan file before calling plan_exit. For an implementation handoff, supply tasks covering every deliverable and its verification. Each task needs a stable key, concrete content, observable criterion and an exact quote from the plan.

Example for a plan containing "Handle duplicate payment retries":
{"tasks":[{"key":"payment-retries","content":"Implement and verify idempotent payment retries","criterion":"Repeated requests charge once","quote":"Handle duplicate payment retries"}]}

The recorded revision and decomposition are presented for approval. After approval, the runtime creates persistent tasks from that revision and switches to Build. Repeating the same approved revision preserves existing task progress. To change its decomposition, revise the plan content and seek approval of that revision. The runtime validates references; you remain responsible for covering every requested result.

A Plan-only Goal records the ready plan without switching to Build and may omit tasks. Resolve questions that prevent a concrete, reviewable plan before requesting approval.
