# Redcode product contract

## Register

product

## Users and purpose

Developers use Redcode in their terminal to investigate, plan, design and implement
software while retaining sessions, project context, goals and automation.

## Reference and identity

The reference experience is Redcode v0.57.0, released September 25, 2026.
Preserve its colors, layout, commands and features while adapting its engine to
OpenCode V2. The shipped product and its independent version remain Redcode.

## Design principles

- Opening Redcode, `/new` and `/clear` opens a usable blank session.
- Keep Build → Plan → Design → Question, with stable agent colors.
- Keep Context visible in the right sidebar and Workers / Subagents in the bottom activity drawer.
- Keep S1/S2 setup, Design feedback, voice, hooks and automation reachable.
- Use real session and service data; unavailable states must remain visible.

## Accessibility and interaction

Preserve keyboard and mouse operation, light/dark and custom themes, narrow and
wide terminal layouts, and the existing animation preference. Retain text labels
alongside colors. No new accessibility certification is asserted by this migration.
